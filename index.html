Stash Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026 · Applies to the Stash app for iPhone and Android

The short version: Stash keeps everything on your phone.
  • No account and no sign-in.
  • We don’t collect, receive, sell or share any of your data. We have no servers that store it.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Speech is recognised on your phone. Your voice is never sent to us.

What Stash stores, and where

Everything you enter stays in the app’s own storage on your device:

  • Places (rooms, shelves, bins) and items, with names, amounts, tags and notes.
  • A history of changes to each item (added, moved, used), which powers undo.
  • Photos you take in the app. They are saved inside Stash, not in your photo library.
  • Your own search words and a few app preferences (like the last bin you used).

The app’s developer cannot see any of it. Deleting the app deletes this data from the device.

Permissions

PermissionWhyWhen it’s asked
CameraTo photograph where you store things and to scan QR labels on bins.The first time you open the camera or scanner.
MicrophoneTo hear voice requests like “where’s my drill?” or “add three screws”.The first time you tap a microphone button.
Speech recognitionTo turn what you say into text, on your device.Together with the microphone.

Stash only listens while you’re using a microphone button. If your phone can’t recognise speech offline, Stash tells you and does not fall back to an online service. Answers that Stash reads aloud are spoken by your phone’s built-in voice.

You can turn any permission off at any time in your phone’s Settings. The rest of the app keeps working.

Backups and exports

  • Your phone’s backup. If you use iCloud Backup (iPhone) or Google’s backup (Android), your phone may include Stash’s data in it. On Android only the item list is included, not photos. These backups are run by Apple or Google under their privacy policies, in your own account. We can’t access them.
  • “Back up now.” Creates a file with your items, a spreadsheet and your photos, and opens your phone’s share sheet. You choose where it goes (for example Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or AirDrop). Stash doesn’t send it anywhere on its own.
  • “Import backup.” Reads a backup file you pick, on your device.

Labels, printing and links

  • QR labels contain only a short random code, never item names or anything about you. Scanning one in Stash looks the code up on your device.
  • Printing and sharing label sheets uses your phone’s system print and share features.
  • If you set up a Siri Shortcut that opens Stash with a question, the question is handled on your device.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising, advertising IDs or tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage statistics.
  • No third-party data processors and no data sales.

Children

Stash is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. Because Stash does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

Your data is yours. You can edit or delete anything in the app, export everything at any time for free, and remove all data by deleting the app (and any backup files you saved).

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that work differently (for example optional sync between devices), this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date. Optional features that send data off your device will always be off until you turn them on.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at github.com/johnybui/stash/issues.
