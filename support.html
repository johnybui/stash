Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Stash works without signing in, and everything stays on your phone.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Search, adding items, photos, labels and voice all work offline.

How do I back up or move to a new phone?

Settings → Back up now saves a file with your items, a spreadsheet and your photos. Save it to Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive. On the new phone, Settings → Import backup. Importing merges and never deletes anything.

Voice says it isn't available

Stash only uses speech recognition that runs on your phone. Some phones or languages don't support that offline; you can still use the keyboard's microphone key in the search box. If you turned off microphone or speech access, turn it back on in your phone's Settings.

How do I print QR labels?

Open a place → Print label. Choose US Letter (30 per sheet), A4 (21 per sheet) or a 62 mm label printer, and print at 100% (actual size).

I deleted something by mistake

Tap Undo in the bar at the bottom within 10 seconds. Deletes and moves can always be undone right away.
